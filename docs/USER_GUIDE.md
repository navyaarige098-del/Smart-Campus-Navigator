# User guide

## Find a place
- **Home → search bar** or the **Places** tab: type a name, room number (e.g. `C-101`) or keyword (`food`, `books`).
- Tap a category chip (Labs, Libraries …) to narrow the list.

## See it on the map
- Open a place to see its building highlighted in red, with floor, room and timings.
- In the **Map** tab, pinch to zoom and tap any building to list the places inside it.

## Get directions
- Tap **Get Directions** on Home, or **Navigate here** on a place.
- Choose *From* and *To*; the shortest route is drawn in blue with distance, walking time and steps.

## Save places
- Tap the bookmark icon on a place. Saved places appear in the **Saved** tab and stay after restarting the app.

## Dark mode
- Tap the moon/sun icon on the Home screen.
